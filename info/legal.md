# Legal, Safety, Copyright & Compliance Documentation

## 1. Primary Safety Disclaimer (Mandatory Display)
The following text appears immediately adjacent to the calculator results, in the footer of every page, and in the technical breakdown drawer:

> **IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE:**  
> DIN Calculator Pro generates estimated binding release values based on the international ISO 11088 standard. These values are provided for educational, informational, and cross-reference purposes only. 
>
> Ski binding release mechanisms directly affect your safety and risk of injury. Actual binding settings depend on boot wear, binding model, friction strip condition, forward pressure, and mechanical calibration. **Never ski on bindings that have not been professionally inspected, adjusted, and torque-tested on calibrated equipment by a certified ski technician.**
